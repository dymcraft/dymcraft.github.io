Be a Pro Tennis Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DYMCraft ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the mobile game Be a Pro Tennis (the "App"). By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or payment card number. The App collects the following information:

  • Anonymous user ID. When you first open the App, it creates a random identifier and stores it on your device. We use it to sign you in to our game server (PlayFab) so that your ranking and PvP data can be saved. It does not contain your name or contact details.
  • Nickname. The nickname you choose in the App is stored on our game server and is shown to other players on the ranking board and in PvP matches.
  • Game data. Your PvP rating, win/loss record, coin total, and the character data used for PvP (stats, skill, and appearance settings) are stored on our game server. Other players may play against a copy of your character (a "ghost") controlled by the computer.
  • Advertising and device information. Our advertising partners, Google AdMob and the ad networks that serve ads through AdMob mediation (currently Unity Ads), may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, and information about how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Purchase information. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, but we do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Consent choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region that requires consent, Google's consent tool may ask for your choices about personalized advertising and store them on your device.

Most of your game progress (for example, your characters, items, and settings) is stored only on your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the game, including sign-in, ranking boards, and PvP matches.
  • To save and display your PvP rating, nickname, and ghost data.
  • To show rewarded advertisements and to measure and improve them (through Google AdMob and its mediation partners).
  • To process and verify in-app purchases (through Google Play).
  • To keep the game fair, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to fix technical problems.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads (advertising network used through AdMob mediation): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Microsoft PlayFab (game server for rankings and PvP): https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising

The App shows rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and by ad networks connected to AdMob, such as Unity Ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your nickname, PvP rating, and ghost data on our game server for as long as your account is active. If you want your server data deleted, email us at dh.yoon@dymcraft.com from the device you play on, or tell us your nickname so that we can identify your account. We will delete it within 30 days, unless we are required by law to keep it. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the App.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us at the email below so that we can delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at dh.yoon@dymcraft.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, and the App communicates with our game server over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. Your continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
